Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

What is Nova Py-Hub?
Nova Py-Hub is a completely free Python learning platform built for beginners through professional developers. We provide structured lessons, hands-on projects, and certificate upon completion — all accessible from your phone or PC.
How are the lessons structured?
Lessons are organized into four levels: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, and Professional. Each level contains 10+ lessons with code examples, explanations, and practical exercises. You progress at your own pace.
Is there any support available if I have questions?
Yes! We provide interactive support through our Q&A system. You can ask questions on any lesson and get help from instructors and the community.
Can I learn on my phone?
Absolutely. Every lesson is designed to be readable and interactive on both PC and phone. You can write and test Python code on your phone using free apps like Termux (Android) or Pythonista (iOS).
Is the certificate really free?
Yes. Once you complete all lessons in a level, you can generate your certificate of completion instantly — completely free. It includes your name, course level, and completion date.
How long does it take to complete?
The full path from beginner to professional typically takes 3-6 months with consistent study (30 minutes to 1 hour daily). You can go faster or slower depending on your schedule.
