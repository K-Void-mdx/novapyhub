✦ FAQ

Everything you need to know

What is Nova Py-Hub?
Nova Py-Hub is a completely free Python learning platform built for beginners through professional developers. We provide structured lessons, hands-on projects, quizzes, and certificate upon completion — all accessible from your phone or PC.
How are the lessons structured?
Lessons are organized into four levels: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, and Professional. Each level contains 10-12 lessons with code examples, explanations, practical exercises, and quizzes. You progress at your own pace.
Is there support available?
Yes! You can reach out via WhatsApp — tap the buttons on the bottom of the page. The owner and assistant are available to help with your questions.
Can I learn on my phone?
Absolutely. Every lesson is designed for both PC and phone. The device selector at the start gives you tailored instructions for iPhone, Android, or PC.
How does the certificate work?
Complete all lessons in a level (pass the quizzes with 60%+) and your certificate is automatically updated. No extra steps needed — just learn and achieve.
How long does it take?
The full path typically takes 3-6 months with consistent study (30 min to 1 hour daily). Go at your own pace — there's no deadline.
